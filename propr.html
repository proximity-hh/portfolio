Story

Why Propr exists

Books list hundreds of props a game, and checking one meant three other tabs. I did everything, from research and design to the front end and the projection model, to build a board that shows only the props worth a look, and says why.

02

Framing

Benchmarked Linemate and sportsbook apps

Bettors don't need more data. They need the noise cut.

Benchmarking showed the gap: B1 Linemate shows everything with no filter, B2 sportsbooks are fast but never say “skip this one”, and B3 late news moves a line before you notice.

How might we

Show tonight's few worthwhile props, and flag the second news changes one?

Not solving: placing bets, listing every prop, or tips without a reason.

40 invited testers. PostHog for activation and retention, Propr's Recap for hit rates

Too many favourites on the board led to v0.0.12, which tightened Trends picks with a −280 odds cap. Testers wanted a quick read, so I added the Basic view. Invites spreading beyond the group led to an access code plus a two-step email code.
