UX case study · Spotify onboarding · 2026

Reducing signup drop-off

Spotify asks for six things before it plays a song. I put the payoff first: a playlist built from your taste, then the account.

RoleSole designer: audit, flows, UI, prototype
Timeline4 weeks, Aug–Sep 2026
TypeSelf-directed, unsolicited
ToolsFigma, FigJam

01

Audit

Spotify's signup today

Drop-off isn't caused by length. It's length that gives nothing back.

Constraint: date of birth is legally required, so it stays.

ServiceStarts withMust collectBurden
YouTube MusicGoogle account and historyNothing
Apple MusicApple ID, no historyTaste
SpotifyNothingIdentity and taste
Tidal, DeezerNothing, plus a paywallIdentity, taste, payment

No major music app shows the payoff before the ask. The closest pattern is Duolingo: a lesson before the account.

02

Drivers

Why people leave, and the response to each

D1Effort with no reward

Six asks before any audio.

Payoff before the ask

D2Unknown length

No progress shown.

Segmented bar from screen one

D3Costly errors

Three validation loops, all before value.

Validate after there's something to protect

D4Asks feel irrelevant

Date of birth arrives unexplained.

Explain every ask

D5Decision fatigue

Artist grid, three-pick minimum.

Fewer options, no minimum

D6Commitment too early

Account before proof.

Prove the product first

William Chen, 24Master's student, just switched from iPhone to Android, 5–6 hours a day on headphones I gave it three artists and it played me the same stuff it plays everyone.
03

Bet

One hypothesis, written to be proven wrong

If

a new user gets a personal playlist before registering,

Then

more people who open the app reach first play,

Because

the account saves something they own, instead of gating something unproven.

Fails if

playlist → account converts no better than Spotify's welcome → account.

“You added three screens.” Yes, and each gives back. So the metric is end-to-end completion, not form completion.

05

Measure

How we'd know, and what's missing

App open→First playPrimary metric. End-to-end, so people who quit early still count.
Time to first playDay-7 retentionSkip rateLost-setup support tickets

Ship rule: if completion rises but day-7 retention falls, it optimised the wrong thing.

Artists ignore genre picks→Build the artist grid from chosen genres
No age gate yet→Test it up front vs. at the end
Skipping still says “picked for your taste”→Separate copy for the starter mix
Picks lost if you leave at the form→Design the abandonment path
No social sign-up→Add Google and Apple at the last step
